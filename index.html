New to UGC? Here's the short version

What is UGC?

User-Generated Content is authentic-feeling video and photos made by a real creator (us!) — then handed straight to you to use on your own channels and ads. It looks like a genuine recommendation from a friend, not a glossy commercial. No influencer following required, no expensive production. Just real content that quietly does the selling for you.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a big following to work with you?

Nope! UGC isn't about our followers — it's content we make for you to post on your own channels and ads. You're hiring our creativity and authenticity, not our audience.

Who owns the content — and can we run it as ads?

You do. Every package includes full usage rights to keep and use the content on your own social channels, website, and email. Paid ads and whitelisting (running it from our handles) are available too — just ask and we'll include the right rights for your plan.

How long does it take?

Most projects are delivered within 1–2 weeks of the brief and receiving your product. Need it faster? Our Brand Ad Bundle includes priority turnaround.

How many revisions do we get?

Every package includes revisions (1–2 rounds depending on the package) so we get it just right. We want you thrilled with the final content.

What makes you different from other UGC creators?

We're a whole family — a faith-first, homeschooling family of six — and both of us are natural on camera. That means authentic couple, kids, and family angles that a single creator simply can't offer. Perfect for family, faith, home, kids, and wellness brands.

What do you need from us to get started?

Just your product and a quick brief — what you're launching, your goal, and any must-have talking points. We handle the concept, filming, and editing from there.
