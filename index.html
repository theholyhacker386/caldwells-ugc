New to UGC? Here's the short version

What is UGC?

User-Generated Content is authentic-feeling video and photos made by a real creator (me!) — then handed straight to you to use on your own channels and ads. It looks like a genuine recommendation from a friend, not a glossy commercial. No influencer following required, no expensive production. Just real content that quietly does the selling for you.
